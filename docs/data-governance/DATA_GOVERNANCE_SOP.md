# Data governance SOP

## Daily (automatic, 7 AM via cron)
- Data checks run; new P0 issues go to the owner by app and SMS, P1 in the app.
- Accountability reminders and escalations run.

## Daily (people)
- Property manager: open Data Center, fix or assign every P0 the same day.
- Guards: meter readings on each round.

## Weekly (Monday)
- Manager: clear P1 issues or note why they wait; verify specs entered last week (second person).
- Operations head: review contractor documents waiting for a check.
- Board (5 minutes): Data Center badge and safety gate.

## Monthly
- Treasurer: bank reconciliation filed by the 10th.
- Manager: renewal list for the next 60 days; start renewals at 45 days.
- Operations head: readiness score and open issues in the monthly board report.

## Quarterly
- Call every emergency contact; update numbers.
- Spot-check 10 assets against their nameplates.

## Yearly
- Counsel and safety officer re-confirm the compliance rules.
- Review contractor requirements, approval limits, retention periods.
- Fire and evacuation drill report filed.

## Changing a master record
1. Enter the change with its source.
2. A second person verifies it.
3. The audit log keeps who, what and when. Never delete a record to "fix" it; retire or correct it.
