# Document and data request checklist

Send this to every new client (condo corporation or property owner) with `Building_Onboarding_Templates.xlsx`. Mark each line Received / Not available / Will follow. "Not available" is a fine answer; a guess is not.

Please send scans (PDF or clear phone photos). Originals stay with you; we only need to see them once to verify.

## A. Property and governance
- [ ] Master deed and declaration of restrictions (with unit floor areas)
- [ ] Certificate of Occupancy
- [ ] SEC registration, by-laws, latest General Information Sheet
- [ ] Board resolution appointing us and naming the signatories
- [ ] House rules and any board-approved policies (with dates)
- [ ] Latest owners' list with unit, floor area, contact (template sheet 02)

## B. Plans and technical documents
- [ ] As-built architectural, structural, electrical, plumbing/sanitary, mechanical and fire protection plans
- [ ] Equipment list from turnover, and any later additions (sheet 03)
- [ ] Manuals and warranties: elevators, generator, fire pumps, fire alarm, transformers, pumps, CCTV, access control
- [ ] Renovation and major repair records (last 5 years)

## C. Maintenance
- [ ] Current maintenance contracts (elevator, fire protection, genset, pest, water tanks)
- [ ] Preventive maintenance schedules and the source of each interval (sheet 04)
- [ ] Last 24 months of repair and maintenance spending (sheet 14)
- [ ] Last inspection reports (elevator, electrical, fire protection, water)

## D. Contractors and suppliers
- [ ] For each contractor: SEC/DTI, mayor's permit, BIR 2303, insurance, licences (sheet 05)
- [ ] Two references for each contractor above ₱50,000 a month
- [ ] Last 24 months of purchases (sheet 15)

## E. Compliance and insurance (confirm with your counsel which apply)
- [ ] Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (BFP)
- [ ] Elevator certificates of operation; annual electrical inspection (Office of the Building Official)
- [ ] Sanitary permit; potable water test results
- [ ] DENR permits: generator permit to operate, wastewater discharge permit, hazardous waste ID if any
- [ ] Fire/all-risk and general liability policies; D&O if any (sheet 11)
- [ ] Emergency and evacuation plan; last drill report
- [ ] Data Protection Officer appointment; NPC registration if applicable; privacy notice

## F. Money
- [ ] Approved budget for this year (sheet 09)
- [ ] Last 12 months of financial statements and bank reconciliations
- [ ] Receivables aging (unpaid dues by unit)
- [ ] Reserve or sinking fund balance and capex plans (sheet 17)
- [ ] Utility bills, last 12 months (sheet 08)

## G. People and safety
- [ ] Emergency contacts (sheet 12)
- [ ] Building staff and on-site contractor personnel, with mobiles (sheet 13). No salary or ID data needed.
- [ ] Security agency post orders and incident procedures

## H. Leasing (if the corporation or owners want our leasing service)
- [ ] Active leases registered with the admin office (sheet 07)
- [ ] Parking allocations

We collect only what management needs. Personal data is processed under our data processing agreement (Schedule C of the management agreement) and the Data Privacy Act.
